Why not just use Windows sharing?

I use local accounts. After every Windows reinstall, I had to dig through settings and forum threads to reach my other PC.

Who can access my files?

Other devices on your private network can read, add or change files in the shared inbox. Passwordless mode relaxes Windows guest and signing protections. Use a network you trust, or choose a sharing account and password. You approve the changes first and can undo them under Advanced.
